App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On iOS, our applications request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA) or performing tracking across other companies’ apps and websites that requires ATT authorization.

If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use permitted information for personalized advertising, advertising measurement, and related purposes.

If you decline, we do not access the IDFA or perform tracking that requires ATT authorization. Other permitted processing, such as non-tracking diagnostics, may still occur.

You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
